Privacy Policy (Datenschutz)

Last Updated: May 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Bürgertest app. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android application.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We leverage industry-standard services to provide a seamless learning experience:

  • Local Offline Storage (Room DB): Your learning progress, bookmarks, and local preferences are stored securely on your device using a local Room Database.
  • Cloud Sync (Firebase Authentication & Firestore): If you choose to create an account, we use Firebase Authentication to manage your login credentials. Your progress data is securely synced to Firebase Firestore to allow cross-device access and backups.
  • AI Explanations (Groq API): When you request an explanation for a question, the question data is sent securely to the Groq API to generate an AI response. We do not transmit personally identifiable information (PII) during this process.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

For users on our Free tier, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data (such as device identifiers and location data) to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

4. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to securely manage our premium subscriptions and lifetime purchases. RevenueCat may process your transaction data to verify subscription status, but they do not receive your direct payment information, which is securely handled by Google Play.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information. Detailed instructions on how to exercise your right to account and data deletion are provided in the section below.

6. Account and Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, we provide a clear and simple method for users to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated data. You may exercise this right at any time.

  • Contact Method: Please send an email to ishara.m.official@gmail.com.
  • Subject Line: Use "Account Deletion Request" as the subject.
  • Requirement: You must send the request from the email address associated with your account to verify your identity.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently and securely delete the following data from our systems within 30 days:

  • Personal Information: Your name and email address.
  • Firebase Authentication Data: Your account credentials and user profile records.
  • App Progress: Your saved learning progress, question history, and study streaks.
  • Financial Info (RevenueCat): Your purchase history and subscription status records.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via the support section in the app or at ishara.m.official@gmail.com.
